# Campaign research digest — 2026-09-03

What the runtime can express, what content ships, and where the story stands,
as surveyed on this date against `main` at `81406c7`. **Dated on purpose**: every
claim below was true when written and none of it is a substitute for the census
in `README.md`. Line numbers drift; grep before quoting them.

## 1. What a mission can express (`data/schemas/mission.schema.json`, `packages/sim/src/mission.ts`)

**Top level** (`additionalProperties: false`): `id name town phase target_minutes
briefing map ledger starting_force resources objectives roe civilians enemy
triggers structures`. Required: `id town phase map objectives ledger`. `town` is
an **enum** in the schema that already lists all six GDD §2 towns (Khan Rafid,
Deir Amun and Umm Zeitoun included), so only a *seventh* town is a schema edit;
every new town still needs a `world.json` entry, and `validate_data.mjs` requires every mission to belong to exactly one town.
**Read by nothing** (lore only): `phase`, `town`, `target_minutes`,
`map.time_of_day`, `enemy.doctrine_profile`, `resources.supply_corridor`.

**Objectives** — `{ id, type, primary, text?, target?, count?, seconds? }`
(`threshold`, `unlocks_phase` are schema-only). Victory = all primaries
complete. Defeat = force wiped, or ROE < `fail_below`, or any primary `failed`.
Secondaries are mechanically inert.

| type | completes | fails |
|---|---|---|
| `locate` | all units with `target` tag identified, else `count` identifications | never |
| `eliminate_hvt` | all units carrying `target` tag dead | never |
| `capture` | `seconds` (10) held **uninterrupted** in zone `target`; contest resets | never |
| `hold_for` | `seconds` (60) held cumulatively in zone `target` | never |
| `survive_until` | tick ≥ `seconds` (60) | never |
| `destroy_all` | every enemy dead | never |
| `raze` | every structure in zone `target` (snapshot at start) destroyed | `seconds` elapsed |
| `collapse` | every tunnel whose mouth is in zone `target` collapsed | `seconds` elapsed |
| `evacuate_before` | `count` civilians reach the refuge | `seconds` (300) elapsed |

`mark`, `escort`, `no_collateral_above`: in the schema, **runtime throws**.
Shipped usage across 14 missions: 36 objectives — `locate` ×10, `hold_for` ×7,
`eliminate_hvt` ×6.

**Triggers** — `{ id?, on: {kind, value?, zone?}, do: {kind, group?, to?, units?} }`,
each fires **once**. `on.kind`: `first_contact` · `casualties_pct` (value = % of
the start-of-mission enemy snapshot dead) · `timer_s` (value = seconds) ·
`zone_entered` (zone; any living **player** unit inside). `do.kind`: `commit`
(attack-move `group` → marker `to`) · `withdraw_to` (move `group` → `to`) ·
`spawn` (placements on the enemy side) · `reinforce` (placements on the player
side) · `dismount` (unload every carrier in `group`). No compound conditions, no
negation, no "objective complete" trigger (that exists only on waves), no
re-arming.

**Waves** — `{ at_seconds, trigger? (an objective id — fires on completion
instead of the clock), to?, units: [{unit, count, from?, group?, tag?}] }`.
`from` resolves as a **map marker** only; the schema's "or tunnel id" is not
implemented.

**Placement** (`enemy.garrisons`, waves, spawn/reinforce) — `unit count at
marker facing_deg group tag in_tunnel digs passengers[] stance{kind, tiles,
waypoints, building}`. **`starting_force` is a separate shape**: `unit count
from_ledger at` only — player units cannot carry `group`, `tag` or `stance`.

**ROE** — `enabled`, `civilian_casualty_penalty` (8), `flagged_structure_penalty`
(5), `disproportionate_ordnance_penalty` (3), `flagged_zones[]`, `fail_below`,
`structure_penalty_mult`. Score starts at 100 and only falls; the reason strings
are engine text, not authorable.

**Civilians** — `{ groups[], refuge }`; the arrival zone must contain the refuge
marker or the runtime throws. **Structures** — `[{type, at, size}]` raised at
start from `data/structures.json`. **Resources** — `logistics_start intel_start
logistics_rate_per_min`.

**Ledger** — five keys exist and the runtime produces exactly these:
`roster.surviving_units` (with veterancy; read by `from_ledger`),
`roe.mission_ratings` (best-of), `campaign.completed_missions` (gates
`unlock.after_mission`), `intel.marked_positions` (placement tags recon
identified; a tagged garrison spawns pre-identified and **forfeits its ambush**),
`civ.settlements_evacuated` (produced once, read by nothing). Unknown `produces`
keys are silently dropped. Unlocks: units gate on `roe_rating_min` /
`after_mission` (9 KDF units); regions gate on `after_mission`. `starting_force`
**ignores** unit unlocks (known hole — field only what the campaign plausibly holds).

**Text fields that reach the player**: `name`, `briefing`, `objectives[].text`.
That is all. Trigger `id` leaks as `enemy reacts (<id>)`.

**Events available to bind a cue.** `MissionEvent`: `objective` (complete/failed),
`trigger`, `wave`, `roe`, `built`, `evacuated`, `missionEnd`. `SimEvent` (24):
`spawn contact fire aps impact component nearMiss ambushSprung strike smokeLaid
revealed structureHit structureDestroyed garrison transport ventOpened
tunnelContact surfaced submerged tunnelCollapsed routed rallied pinned unpinned
destroyed`. The tutorial's `await` already gates on any of them. Reaching a
`SimEvent` from a mission trigger means a new `on.kind` in `stepTriggers` —
engine work, small and local. Anything per-speaker, branching, sequenced or
repeatable is real engine work.

**Economy and AI, as shipped.** `requestBuild` spends logistics/intel and deploys
at the `camp` or `player_start`; `buildBlockedReason` names the unlock. Satellite
sweep 150, precision strike 250. Enemy targeting is engine: `hurts`-first, then
nearest. "Commander runs under pressure" is `casualties_pct: 40 → withdraw_to`
(`wadi_halam_3_counterraid`). Not authorable: stance changes mid-mission,
re-arming triggers, morale-conditioned retreat, target priorities, formations,
anything the enemy says.

## 2. What ships (census 2026-09-03)

**Units — 30 types, every one draws.** Faction enum `kdf | ashwar | sarim | rif |
civilian`. Mesh authority `packages/app/src/mesh-catalogue.ts`; sprite authority
`SPRITE_MAP` in `packages/app/src/main.ts`. Six are sprite-only (no GLB):
`attack_drone recon_drone gun_truck loiter_drone manpad_team recoilless_team`.
`civilians` is mesh-only (four variants; draws nothing on Pixi or `&nomesh`).
`heli_peten` is fielded by no mission.

| KDF (`data/units/kdf/`) | unlock | Ashwar | Sarim | Rif |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `inf_squad`, `at_team`, `mortar_team`, `apc_eitan`, `jeep_shoded` | — | `militia_cell`, `rpg_team`, `mortar_crew`, `digger_crew`, `charge_squad`, `paramotor` (air) | `sarim_rifles`, `atgm_cell`, `recoilless_team`, `manpad_team`, `rocket_battery` (Grad, range 20, sight 6), `loiter_drone` (air) | `technical`, `moto_rpg`, `gun_truck` |
| `recon_drone` 35 · `ifv_namer` 40 · `attack_drone` 45 · `demo_squad` 50 · `mbt_lavi` 55 · `yahalom_squad` 55 · `sniper_team` 60 · `dozer_d9` 60 · `heli_peten` 65 | ROE ≥ | | | |

**Structures — 8 types, each with idle + wreck GLB** (`data/structures.json`,
`art/meshes/buildings/`): `shanty s · house h · apartment a · warehouse w ·
concrete # · wall = · mosque m (roe_penalty 30) · camp c` (production; placed by
`structures[]`, only `beit_sahwan_2_foothold` does). Only `house`, `apartment`,
`warehouse` have photo-textured Meshy sources; the other five are kit-built.

**Decor families** (`decor-place.ts`): grass, sand, bush (cover), tree (`o`),
rock (`n`), slab (`^`), boulder (`b`), ditch (`d`, one variant). Road `r` has none.

**Maps — six, all 48×48** (`data/maps/`): `tutorial_ground`,
`beit_sahwan_outskirts` (4 missions), `marj_perimeter` (First Light),
`tel_marum` (relief, 3 missions), `wadi_halam_basin` (`green`, 5 missions),
**`qarn_hadid`** (relief 0–7, all ten symbols, a village, two gates — **no
mission uses it**). Terrain enum `arid | green`. Symbols: `. 1 2 3 r o n ^ b d`
plus building letters.

**VFX** — 15 emitters in `data/vfx/`; three mesh VFX GLBs. **Audio** — 11 sets,
all weapons/impacts, 31 `.ogg` + 31 `.m4a`, all CC0 generated by
`tools/gen_audio.py`. **No voice, dialogue, narration or music exists.**
`validate:audio` fails on any undeclared file.

**UI art** — `assets/ui/menu_banner.jpg` only. Campaign board: `world_map.png`,
three region layers, `sahar_basin.svg`, `flag_brigade.png` (the only insignia),
`art/meshes/campaign/sahar_basin.glb`. Fonts: Barlow, Big Shoulders Display, IBM
Plex Mono. **No portraits** (`ui/portrait.ts` crops a sprite frame as a stand-in),
no faction insignia, no briefing art, no commander image.

**Gates** — `validate:data` (schemas, map bounds, palette), `validate:assets`
(sprites: palette, reserved bands, binary alpha, silhouette IoU, framing),
`validate:meshes` (every GLB rendered headlessly in Blender, same checks;
`house/apartment/warehouse` palette-exempt), `validate:ui` (no colour literals),
`validate:audio`, cost-curve + `pnpm balance`, `test:determinism`, the visual
golden gate. **No gate** on `assets/ui`, `assets/campaign`, `assets/textures`,
fonts, or mesh provenance. Rules: CC BY-SA 4.0 for art/data, DCO sign-off,
AI-generated assets permitted with PR disclosure, paid packs banned, no real
identity attached to a faction.

## 3. Where the story stands

**Canon (GDD §2).** Kedem, KDF 401st "Ari'im" Brigade. Sahar Basin: Marj Strip
(west, coastal, Ashwar Front — Beit Sahwan, Khan Rafid, Deir Amun); Sur (north,
mountains, Sarim Brigades — Tel Marum, Umm Zeitoun); Naharin (east, green basin,
Rif Cells — Wadi Halam). War order: *proximity, then standoff, then source*.
`countries.json` also names six regions with no fiction at all: Amar Steppe,
Rimon Hills, Kharat Badlands, Zol Erg, Milh Flats (and Kedem at the centre).

**Shipped missions — 14.** Beit Sahwan: `_0_tutorial` (10 min, no ledger),
`_breach` "First Light", `_1_recon`, `_2_foothold`, `_3_clearance`,
`_4_subterranean`. Tel Marum: `_1_recon` "The Gateway", `_2_foothold` "The Start
Line", `_3_clearance` "The Pass". Wadi Halam: `_1_fords`, `_2_laager` "Grazing
Ground", `_3_counterraid` "The Cattle Track", `_4_village`, `_5_depot` "Break the
Depot". Towns with no missions: Khan Rafid, Deir Amun, Umm Zeitoun. Both Sur and
Naharin unlock after `beit_sahwan_3_clearance`.

**Named people.** Shipped: the HUD commander constant, now `Lt Col Shai Hammai`
(`ui/hud.ts:68`, renamed 2026-09-03 from `Lt Col Dagan`; hard-coded because the
schema has no `speaker`). Spec-only: *Colonel Idit Zohar, "Ari Actual"*
(`2026-08-21-commander-brief-design.md`, unbuilt). In data: an enemy group
`amir` / trigger `amir_runs` in Wadi Halam III — "the local commander", never
named to the player.

**Specs approved and unbuilt.** `dispatch` / `aftermath` fields and the `planned`
town flag (storyline spec, 2026-08-21); `data/campaign/commander.json` and the
portrait (commander brief spec). What did ship from that pair: `briefingBeats`
and the in-mission commander bar. **The written ending is superseded**: it made
Sur "the unfinished war", then Tel Marum shipped; `2026-08-22-sur-front-design.md`
says cutting Naharin becomes decisive once Sur is contained, and no replacement
ending text exists. Umm Zeitoun was planned there as four missions (recon,
build-up, clearance, "the batteries themselves").

**The voice on record** (all 14 briefings): second-person imperative from a
superior, present tense, concrete tile-level fact, closing on a cost. *"Bring
back the picture, not casualties."* *"Do not chase what runs."* *"Mind what you
level — the mosque is not a target."* ROE as consequence: *"you will be billed
for every second they can see it."* No prowords anywhere.

**Open issues touching this work.** GH-94 storyline epic · GH-114 briefing and
debrief screens (blocked on 94) · GH-110 voice and announcement layer · GH-133
music · GH-134 SFX library · GH-135 generative video for cinematics · GH-122 Sur
content (Umm Zeitoun map + missions) · GH-19 Khan Rafid and Deir Amun · GH-22
campaign progression · GH-16 Rif roster · GH-18 surrender and prisoners · GH-2 /

## 4. Decisions the lead took on 2026-09-03

- The protagonist is **Shai Hammai**, an officer who begins the war as a company
  commander at First Light and is promoted across the campaign to Colonel
  (spoken as "a five-star colonel" — the KDF's own insignia, fictional).
- **Idit**, an intelligence officer who was in the same compound at First Light,
  grows beside him and provides the intel in every mission. Shai and Idit are the
  two voices of the HUD.
- **One villain per front**, introduced by an atrocity at the start of the
  front's campaign and captured or killed at its end.
- The GDD needs a story section; `narrative-designer` keeps it.
- **EVA announcements, voice audio and a radio overlay are approved targets.**
- The level scripter proposes **in-level twists** that turn the plot (a kidnapped
  civilian killed, a soldier abducted), classified by what the runtime can do.
- Spellings decided the same day: **Shai Hammai** (heard as Hammai / Hammai /
  Hammami; the lead chose Hammai) and **Idit Zohar**. Villains carry personal
  names in the towns' fictional register: Nadir Sahim, Karim Adhal, Jubran Hallaq.

# The Long Ground: dossier of the real record

**Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Author:** `campaign-historian` · **Status:** working document for
designers. **Never game text.** Nothing in this file may be copied into a mission file, a
briefing, a `say` line, an objective label or any other surface a player sees.
`transposition.md` and `arc-outline.md` cite this file only by the beat IDs below
(P1-c, R2, T3 and so on) and never by the names in it.

**What this is.** It breaks the public operational record of the 2023-2025 Gaza war into
phases and beats, so the design chain can borrow its *shape*. The lead's brief
(`roaring-lions-ep/_coord/campaign-arc/lead-brief-2026-11-15.md`) listed five phases, four
rescue raids and a set of tunnel problems. Each is checked here against a source fetched
or searched on 2026-11-15.

**Rules applied in this file.**
- No casualty figures, for either side. They are contested, and the design does not need them.
- No captive is named, here or anywhere else. The rescue raids are described by operational
  shape only: time of day, building type, number of sites, cover, extraction. Source links
  were picked so that none carries a captive's name.
- A contested claim is attributed to whoever made it, with the dispute beside it. Most
  claims about what lay under a structure are claims by one side only.
- Every person in the record is left unnamed, leaders included. Places, formations and
  operation names do appear, because designers need them to read the sources. They must
  never reach `transposition.md` or `arc-outline.md`: §9 is the deny-list the
  PR was checked against.

**How strong the sourcing is.** Wikipedia articles were fetched directly. Their coverage is
partial: each fetch read the first 100,000 characters of a long page. Other facts come from
search-result summaries of news reports (AP, AFP, NPR, Times of Israel, Al-Monitor, Al
Jazeera, CBS, NBC), the U.S. Army Center for Army Lessons Learned, and the Long War
Journal. Where sources disagree, both versions are given. Before a fact is used as anything
more than shape, read the linked source itself.

**What changed tactically by the end of the phase.** The war moved from entry and encirclement
(open ground, axes, a ring) to block-by-block clearance and below-ground raids. The corridor
became a permanent feature that later phases rested on. The truce was the war's only planned
pause in which captives came back.

## 6. The four rescue raids, by operational shape only

|---|---|---|---|

Four captives were recovered. **Disputed:** whether disguised vehicles were used, and the harm to civilians (figures omitted). | [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2024/06/israel-army-says-rescued-4-hostages-complex-gaza-operation), [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/world/israel-hostage-rescue-what-we-know-rcna156937) |
| **R4** | 27 Aug 2024 | **A captive found alone in a tunnel** in the south by naval commandos, combat engineers and security-service officers searching the complex. There was no resistance, and the captors appeared to have abandoned the captive. Per the army spokesman, the meeting happened underground. It was reported as the first live rescue from a tunnel. | [Long War Journal](https://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2024/08/idf-rescues-a-hostage-in-gaza.php), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/8/27/israeli-military-says-hostage-rescued-from-gaza) |

**The pattern across the four.** None was a set-piece assault on a fortress. All four came out
of intelligence work, and three were above ground in inhabited buildings. The covering fire
that made the extraction possible is the same thing that put the neighbourhood at risk. That
is the design problem the game's Conduct system already models.

## 7. Tunnel warfare

| ID | topic | the record | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | scale | Israeli officials put the network at about 560 to 720 km with about 5,700 shafts, up from an earlier official 400 km. The organisation's leader claimed more than 500 km in 2021. A geomorphologist said it is hard to map accurately from the surface. Every figure is an estimate. | [Ground News, NYT summary](https://ground.news/article/hamas-gaza-tunnels-at-least-350-miles-long-officials), [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/mena/palestine-israel/2023/10/31/robots-and-attack-dogs-what-israel-brings-to-tunnel-combat) |
| **T2** | tiers | The CALL study describes **two tiers**. Tactical tunnels lie near the surface, for movement, ambush, rocket launches and escape. Strategic tunnels lie deeper and are better built, with sleeping quarters, command centres and storage, and carry integrated power, ventilation and communications. The tiers are not all connected. Shafts are usually hidden inside buildings. | [U.S. Army CALL, 10 Sep 2025](https://www.army.mil/article/288356/subterranean_operations_israeli_defense_force_lessons_from_gaza) |
| **T3** | detection and mapping before entry | Layered detection: seismic and acoustic sensors (weak in sandy soil), airborne and ground thermal, ground-penetrating radar, imagery, human intelligence, and fused data. **The sequence:** secure the shaft area against observation and fire, find the vents and other openings, clear explosives, lower a camera, then map with dogs and camera robots, and only then send a team in. Smoke pumped in reveals the nearby openings. Drones were adapted to fly inside buildings and tunnels. | [U.S. Army CALL](https://www.army.mil/article/288356/subterranean_operations_israeli_defense_force_lessons_from_gaza), [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/mena/palestine-israel/2023/10/31/robots-and-attack-dogs-what-israel-brings-to-tunnel-combat), [Jerusalem Post](https://jpost.com/arab-israeli-conflict/gaza-news/article-773545) |
| **T4** | flooding | The WSJ reported in early Dec 2023 that pumping seawater had begun, with at least five pumps north of a coastal camp; U.S. officials raised concerns about the aquifer. The army later confirmed flooding some tunnels and said the method did not suit every tunnel. A later WSJ report said it was not producing the expected results. CALL: **largely ineffective** (porous soil, blast doors, drainage) and largely discontinued. | [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/idf-confirms-flooding-hamas-tunnels-in-gaza-with-seawater/), [Jerusalem Post, citing WSJ](https://jpost.com/breaking-news/article-777762), [U.S. Army CALL](https://www.army.mil/article/288356/subterranean_operations_israeli_defense_force_lessons_from_gaza) |
| **T5** | command-bunker raids | Under a hospital (P1-e, contested). Under the southern part of the main city (P1-g, army claim). Under an aid agency's headquarters (P1-i, contested). A vehicle-wide tunnel near a crossing (P1-h, army claim). | see the beats |
| **T6** | border-corridor demolition | Shafts under the border corridor, some reportedly crossing the border (P3-c, contested), were found and demolished as the corridor was taken (P3-b). | see the beats |
| **T7** | demolition methods | Armoured bulldozers or other engineering plant for physical collapse, which needs area security. Polyurethane foam barriers. Bunker-busting bombs on fortified sections. Controlled charges set by the counter-tunnel engineers, the method chosen by soil type. | [U.S. Army CALL](https://www.army.mil/article/288356/subterranean_operations_israeli_defense_force_lessons_from_gaza) |
| **T8** | buffer zones and dividing corridors | More than 600 buildings were demolished around the dividing corridor's road over about three months (NYT, via Israel Hayom), and the border buffer was roughly doubled, to as much as 3 km in places, per a military map. Estimates of its share of the territory vary widely, from 26% to 50% or more. Israel calls the measures temporary pressure; critics (HRW) argue long-term control. **For designers:** the real clearing reportedly took homes and farmland. The fiction's demolition missions deliberately do not (`arc-outline.md`, the dozer mission). | [Israel Hayom, citing the NYT](https://israelhayom.com/2024/12/02/nyt-israel-built-military-bases-in-gaza), [Yahoo / AP](https://www.yahoo.com/news/israel-controls-50-gaza-palestinians-061014519.html) |
| **T9** | the engineers | A counter-tunnel special engineering unit (founded 1995) and naval commando detachments were attached directly to manoeuvre brigades. A canine unit reconnoitred tunnels and searched for explosives. | [U.S. Army CALL](https://www.army.mil/article/288356/subterranean_operations_israeli_defense_force_lessons_from_gaza) |

## 8. Where the brief and the record disagree

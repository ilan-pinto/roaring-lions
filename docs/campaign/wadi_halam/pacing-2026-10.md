## The instrument: dead air

- A second counts as **engaged** when a `fire` event passes between a side-0 unit and a side-1
  unit, in either direction, or when an objective changes state in it. Rounds fired at buildings
  or at civilians do not count.
- Engaged seconds less than 10 s apart are one **fight**. A reload is part of a fight. The 10 s is
  `REGEN_DELAY_TICKS` (200 ticks): after that long without damage, field recovery starts and the
  last fight stops costing the player anything. This is the third cause in GH-90.
- **Dead air** is every second outside a fight. "Hold dead / len" counts only the hold window: from
  the first tick the primary `hold_for` clock counts until it completes, or until the run ends.
- Scripted orders are never counted as activity.

Falsified: with `FIGHT_GAP_S` set to 1000, II's optimal run reads 2% dead air instead of 63%. For
calibration, `beit_sahwan_breach` played passive (the siege, where the enemy comes to you) reads
27% dead air, a longest dead stretch of 39 s, and 3.3 fights of 70 s each (`WH_CAL=beit_sahwan_breach`).

## The proposal (JSON)

| mission | spawn times, before | spawn times, after | enemy units spawned |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | 90, 210, 225 | 90, 135, 170, 210, 225, 265 | 6 -> 10 |
| II | 60, 150, 240, 330 | 30, 45, 80, 105, 140, 170, 205, 240, 265, 290, 320, 350 | 10 -> 29 |
| III | 90, 200 | 45, 130, 170, 205, 240, 275, 305 | 5 -> 10 |
| V | 45, 100, 160, 220 | 45, 100, 160, 220, **265, 300** + `reinforce` demo_squad at 75 s | 12 -> 16 (+1 friendly) |

- **I.** Three light waves (one or two motorcycles each) fill the 90 -> 210 s gap and the tail.
  The last one lands at 265 s, on purpose. The optimal plan's last re-anchor is at 310 s. With a
  wave added at 300 s it went 9/12 with 2 runs stuck, and with one at 335 s it went 4/12 with 7
  stuck: a late wave pulls the force off the ford after the plan has stopped bringing it back.
- **II.** Raids every 25-35 s from 30 s. Each vehicle raid is followed by a foot echelon from the
  nearer hide or the well. The foot units (`militia_cell`, `rpg_team`) are what lengthen the
  fights: on vehicles alone (v1, 7 raids), dead air only fell from 86% to 76%. The east track
  still opens at 240 s, so Idit's "third compass point in four minutes" stays true. Raids continue
  to 350 s, because the measured worst case of v2 was the last 90 s of the hold with no enemy
  left. Eight new triggers, each with a label of 48 characters or less. Spawn markers are real,
  and the well spawn is moved off its blocked tile to (24.5, 39.5).
- **III.** Seven small parties in place of two waves, all vehicles, alternating east and
  south: one technical at 45 s, then nothing until 130 s, then a party every 35-40 s to 305 s.
  The heaviest party (two motorcycles and a technical) is the 170 s one, and it carries Idit's
  pump-house line. Two earlier versions were thinned: one with 11 waves cut the sensible force to
  a single APC in 2 seeds of 12, and the ten-echelon first proposal ran the clock to 6.2/6.5 min.
- **V.** Two tail waves at 265 s and 300 s, so the hold does not go quiet after 220 s. The
  **lost-dozer fallback** is a `reinforce` trigger: a second `demo_squad` at `kdf_crossing` at
  75 s, labelled "Second engineer team comes up the road". It arrives whether or not the D9 lives.
  Today's schema has no unit-lost condition, so a conditional fallback cannot be written. The raze
  deadline stays at 300 s: it never binds in a normal run (slack 145-192 s), and with the second
  team every D9-loss probe wins inside it.

## Findings beyond the proposal

1. **#93's first claim does not reproduce on today's main.** "Three primaries do not fit the time
   budget" predates GH-382's new ground. Measured: 12/12 on both sensible and optimal, in 5.1-5.7
   min. The hold clock is also NOT serial with the raze. `hold_for` counts whenever a player unit
   stands in the zone uncontested, and nothing makes it wait for the raze. The objective text
   "Hold the depot for four minutes once it is down" described a rule the runtime does not have;
   it is rewritten (see "Text rewritten").
   **#93's second claim does reproduce:** losing the D9 wins 3-5/12, because the engineers alone
   die before they start.
2. **Naive loses every Wadi Halam hold for a structural reason, before and after.** It ignores the
   second primary: the raze in II and V, the evacuation in I and III, the HVT in III. That makes
   the naive -> sensible step a cliff in every mission. The changes here do not alter that.
3. **Naive runs at the cap are not mission hangs.** I (8/12) and III (8-9/12) sit at 20 minutes
   because the naive force chases a broken wave off the zone and never comes back. The hold clock
   shows "unheld" and can always resume. III adds a second cause: `kill_amir` is an
   `eliminate_hvt` primary with no deadline, so a player who never hunts the commander after he
   withdraws to `rif_east` is never told the mission is lost. The commander stays reachable, so
   this is winnable, not stuck. Still, it is the one primary in the arc that cannot fail.
4. **IV's sensible rung is at the cap in 11/12, before and after.** That is the `wh_ladder` house
   walk stalling on IV, not a pacing question. IV is not in scope.
5. **Outside this arc:** `beit_sahwan_breach`'s passive control wins 2 seeds of 12; see "Open
   items".

## Design read (judgment, not measurement)

> The arc's holds were waiting games: II's fights lasted 6 s with 42 s between them, and the force
> finished at 91% HP. The proposal turns that into a fight every 20 s that costs a fifth of the
> force's HP, with the longest quiet stretch under 40 s. The price is three times the enemy volume
> on II, and a worst seed 0.7 min past its target. III could keep only half its gain under a
> 6-minute clock: its clock already ran out at 5.9 on main, so every extra wave there costs time it
> does not have. I needed it least, and its gain is small. V's real problem was never pacing: one bulldozer was a single point of failure, and the
> 75 s engineer team removes it.

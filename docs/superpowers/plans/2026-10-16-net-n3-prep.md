## 0. Three facts that shape everything below

- **A faction belongs to a seat, not to a side** (§2.2).
  - The host picks `mirror`, `kdf` or `sarim` (`packages/net/src/config.ts`: `Matchup`,
    `factionsOfMatchup`). Today a host who picks Sarim plays Sarim on **side 0**.
  - Every F-plan, by contrast, reads side 0 as "the player" and side 1 as "the authored enemy".
  - So whatever N3 keys by side must work for both factions. Lead decision LD1 below can narrow
    that.
- **Lane C's order is now F1 → F3 → F2 → N2 → F4 → vehicle spacing**, with one sim plan in flight.
  - This order is from N2's own plan ("Sequencing"). It supersedes L5 and the stage-4 calendar.
  - F4 was written before N2 existed, and nothing in it mentions `Command`s or seats.
- **Neither KDF-v-Sarim map has a tunnel or a camp.** The two maps are `FACTION_MATCHUP_MAPS` =
  `tel_marum` and `wadi_tarfan`. Both carry zero `tunnels`, and no map has ever carried a camp tile.
  - `wadi_tarfan` already names the zones a versus rules file would use: `camp_0`/`camp_1`, three
    fords, two bluffs and two farms per side.

## 3. Lead decisions needed

Each item gives the recommendation first, then the alternative and its cost.

- **LD1. Which side is KDF in a faction matchup.**
  - **Recommend:** KDF is always side 0. The host's pick sets which seat is KDF; the side does not
    follow the seat.
  - Conduct, ROE, intel, `from_ledger` and `unitLost` then stay on today's side-0 rule with no
    re-key. N3 builds side 1's human paths, which it must build anyway.
  - It costs the host the choice of map end, unless the spawn anchor is chosen apart from the side.
    That matters: #596 measured fairness by spawn order.
  - *Alternative:* key everything by the KDF seat's side (D1's full re-key). About one N3 task more.
- **LD2. Who authors the doctrine data.**
  - **Recommend:** N3 authors a minimal schema-validated `data/doctrines/{kdf,sarim}.json`: menu,
    budget ratio (Sarim 1.6×, G3 Q7), `pop_cap` and start purse. WP-G-G extends it with the
    commander.
  - This removes N3's hard dependency on Stage 5.
  - *Alternative:* wait for G-G (the spec's "with WP-G-G", Jan–Feb 2027).
- **LD3. The Sarim menu for N3.**
  - **Recommend:** `VERSUS_SARIM`'s five types plus `recoilless_team`.
  - Leave `loiter_drone` out: population 0, which the cap never refuses (F3 C-R8), plus kamikaze,
    makes a spam risk with no balance behind it.
  - Leave the Ashwar and Rif extras (`technical`, `moto_rpg`, …) for §6.3.
  - The KDF menu in a `matched` room: `VERSUS_KDF`'s types. A `brigade` room's `unlocks` stay `[]`
    until this lands (§7.1).
- **LD4. Tunnel orders (§6.1, "into a mouth and out at a vent").**
  - **Recommend:** defer to §6.3 beside `dig`. Neither matchup map has a route, and decision 9 lists
    only "KDF verbs + `setStance` + `build`".
  - *Alternative:* one new `Command` and a containment test in N3.
- **LD5. Fire support in versus.**
  - **Recommend:** off for both seats in N3. `validateCommand` keeps dropping `callStrike`, `reveal`
    and `support`.
  - A KDF-only menu with no Sarim answer would be an untested balance lever. Sarim's menu is §6.3.
- **LD6. Ask Lane C to fold the seat shape into F4.**
  - **Recommend:** yes. F4 keys `requestBuild`, `cancelBuild`, `setRally`/`clearRally`,
    `firstRefusal`, the spend and `built` by side. It lands line, cancel and rally as `Command`
    fields, with the net whitelist in the same PR.
  - F4 edits exactly these functions, and its economy re-pin covers them.
  - *Alternative:* N3 re-keys them afterwards. That is a second pass and a second economy re-pin,
    about two N3 tasks.
- **LD7. Conduct in versus.**
  - **Recommend:** computed for the KDF seat only, report-only. It never ends a match (decision 24
    decides) and it is never shown to the Sarim seat (§6.1).
- **LD8. "The lobby's side pick live" (§9 N3 row).**
  - **Recommend:** the create-time matchup pick (#589) is the pick. N3 makes it **live**, meaning it
    offers the KDF-v-Sarim pairing with the economy on. It adds no `lobby.set` side swap.
  - *Alternative:* an in-lobby swap, which is protocol and Worker work.

## 4. Dependency order and the earliest N3 start

1. **F2 merges** (Task 10 and the final wave left). This fixes the purse, zone and hash shapes N3
   builds on.
2. **N2** (PR #653's plan, cut after F2): the seat table, `setStance` hold/ambush, `build`/`support`
   commands, and `digest()`.
3. **F4**, carrying LD6 if the lead rules for it. Its Task 1 halts for the lead (G-NUM).
4. **N3's sim tasks.** In order:
   1. the versus rules file and doctrine data (LD2);
   2. the runtime booted in rooms, with the digest live;
   3. the `humanHeld` skips (N2 Task 8's design);
   4. side 1's human build path, unless F4 already took it;
   5. `setStance patrol`;
   6. the Conduct seat (LD1, LD7);
   7. `checkEnd` against `outcome.ts`.
5. **N3's app work:** the dock, purse and pop by `viewerSide`; `build` admitted in versus; the
   lobby (LD8).
6. **Gate:** Ilan plays five KDF v Sarim matches (§9).

  the zones and camps on `tel_marum`/`wadi_tarfan`, and the HUD and lobby mocks.

    November 2026**. This is reasoned, not measured.

    rather than waiting for WP-G-G.

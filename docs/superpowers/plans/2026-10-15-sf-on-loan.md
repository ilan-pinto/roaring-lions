## Global Constraints

- App only; never `packages/sim`, never `data/missions`. The sim pins, `pnpm balance` and `pnpm playtest`
  must not move against `feat/gf3-pop-cap`'s own.
- Stamp: display face via CSS capitals (VR-20), `--t-xs`, `--ink-dim` text with a 1 px `--ink-dim`
  outline. Not `--on`, `--selected`, `--info` or `--commend`. Never on the chip's combat-state status
  line; never collides with the kit sign (chip art top-right), the status mark (bottom-left) or the role
  badge (card frame top-left).
- A chip shows the stamp when ANY unit in it is lent; its tooltip says "{n} of {count} on loan" for every
  stamped chip (Q3), followed by the reason line. Chips are never split.
- The dock tile is never marked "on loan" (Q7 ruling).
- Population: used/cap in the dock header beside credits, `--info`, a dim word, no glyph; the warn tone
  at the cap; hidden when `populationView()` is null (every campaign mission today). Not on the strip.
- `capped` tile state: below a campaign lock in precedence (exactly `popCapped`'s semantics),
  `data-capped='1'`, `--disabled-opacity`, `cursor: not-allowed`, short label "pop full", full line with
  the numbers (Q6), shown as one `.rl-warn` line in the dock's rich tip (Q7) and on `title`/`aria-label`.
- `ui.deny` on EVERY locked, capped or refused dock click (ruling 8), through #628's `onDeny`.
- Strings through `t()`; tables hold keys; `pnpm validate:ui` passes; chrome-register/type-register pins
  updated for new classes. Every new check is seen red by a one-line mutation.

## Review Focus

   mark all visible, no overlap; the status line unchanged. Task 1.
2. **A long lent name at 1400x900** ("Shmamit Deep Recon Team") — the chip does not wrap or grow. Task 1
   (asserted structurally: the stamp is not inside the name's text flow).

   deny cue plays. Task 3.

   a drone (population 0) is never capped. Task 3.
5. **A mission with no lent unit and no cap** (most of the campaign) — no stamp anywhere, no population
   readout, no deploy-hint change. Tasks 1–3.

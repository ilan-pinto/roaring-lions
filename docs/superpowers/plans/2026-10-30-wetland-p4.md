# Wetland P4, wet ground and air: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On a `wetland` map the ground is wet and the air is raining. Control C carries the marsh water and the
puddle masks. The ground shader draws marsh water, puddles, a darker rim, ripples and wet roughness under a
`THEME_WET` define. The light passes through `rainLight`, and the haze thickens. Units, foliage, decals and the
vehicle spray take their wet values. Then one instanced batch draws the falling rain and its splashes, on the sim
clock, with a count ladder per preset, and reduced motion turns the motion off. The phase ends on **the cost run**
(spec section 6, all three presets, with the ladder stepped if over) and **the lead's look at the rain**.

**Nothing shipped moves.** No shipped map rains, so arid, green and highland compile today's ground, fog, foliage and
decal programs, boot today's light and build today's scene graph, byte for byte. Task 1 extends the 9.1 pins to
cover the new surfaces **before** any wiring lands, and every later commit keeps them green.

**Architecture:**
- **One switch.** Everything P4 adds keys on `RendererOptions.weather === 'rain'`. `rendererOptionsFor` sets that
  only for `wetland` (P2's `TERRAIN_WEATHER`). The renderer never reads a theme id.
- **`packages/render/src/three/`** gains:
  - `weather.ts` (new, no `three` import): `rainLight`, and the rain, ripple and wet-surface constants;
  - `fog-pass.ts`: an optional `hazeLow` constructor option, interpolated into the source, defaulting to `HAZE_LOW`;
  - `lighting.ts`: nothing new; `ThreeRenderer`'s light boot passes the lights through `rainLight` under rain.
- **`packages/render/src/three/terrain/`** gains:
  - `wet-masks.ts` (new, pure): the control-C bake (R: marsh water from `marshWaterAt`; G: puddles);
  - `wet-shading.ts` (new, pure): the TS mirror of the `THEME_WET` GLSL, which tests drive;
  - `control-map.ts`: an optional third texture, built only when the input is wet, with its splice;
  - `mesh.ts`: `GroundMaterial({ wet })`, the `THEME_WET` block, and the debug stashes for `wet`, `marsh` and
    `ripples`;
  - `sway.ts`: a wet variant of the sway GLSL (amplitude x1.5, gust 1.8), compiled only under rain.
- **`packages/render/src/three/units/`** gains:
  - `rain-maths.ts` (new, pure), `rain-batch.ts` (new): the drop lattice, the fall, the splash and the height bake;
  - `render-order.ts`: `RAIN_RENDER_ORDER`;
  - `unit-surface.ts`: `WET_SURFACE_PARAMS`.
- **`packages/render/src/three/decal-pool.ts`, `decal-maths.ts`:** tread and tyre take `mud.3` under rain, and a
  crater bowl below its height threshold takes the water surface (a decal-program define, rain only).
- **`packages/render/src/quality.ts`:** `RenderQuality.rain?` (drop count, ripples, open puddles) and `RAIN_LADDER`.
- **`packages/render/src/three/debug-layers.ts`:** `rain`, `wet`, `marsh`, `ripples`. (`cloud-shade` is P6.)
- **`data/vfx/vehicle_mud.json`**, registered in `packages/data/src/index.ts`'s `vfxEmitters`.
- **`tools/`** gains:
  - the extended `theme-identity.test.ts` (a third digest, `scene`);
  - `wetland-water-readability.test.ts`, with literal oracles;
  - `ground:capture` options for the two looks;
  - `perf:fx --breakdown` options for the cost run.

## What the code says, and what it changes about the spec's picture

Each point was read at `7aa1db5f`. Read them before any task.

1. **`weather` is a seam with no reader.** `RendererOptions.weather?: 'clear' | 'rain'` (`api.ts`), spread by
   `rendererOptionsFor` only when `TERRAIN_WEATHER[map.terrain] === 'rain'`, so a shipped map's options carry no
   `weather` key at all. `theme-identity.test.ts` has a `weather: 'clear'` arm per shipped theme that cannot go red
   until something reads the field. P4's `rainLight` is the first reader, and the arm becomes live.
2. **`day` is not built from the preset table.** `ThreeRenderer`'s constructor passes `DAY_LIGHTS` (`lighting.ts`'s
   own constants) for day and `presetLights(row)` for dawn and dusk, then `withLightOverride`. So the spec's
   `rainLight(preset)` cannot act on a `LightPreset` row alone: it takes the RESOLVED lights plus the preset's haze
   fields (R-2). `time-of-day.test.ts` and `lighting.test.ts` keep day bit-identical.
3. **`HAZE_LOW` is a literal in the fog-pass GLSL** (`fog-pass.ts`, the `rlHaze` line interpolates
   `HAZE_LOW.toFixed(4)`), not a uniform. And `theme-identity.test.ts` records `hazeLow` from the TS import, not
   from the pass. So rain's 0.10 is a compile-time option of `FogOfWarPass`, and the test must read the pass's own
   value (Task 2), or a wet `HAZE_LOW` would be invisible to the pin.
4. **The shadow box is view-fitted on every preset** since GH-705 (`QUALITY_PRESETS.*.shadowFit: 'view'`). The
   spec's estimate for the shadow-map step's saving (-0.10 to -0.50 ms) predates that. It is measured, not assumed.
5. **The ground material is `roughness: 1`, `metalness: 0`, built with no arguments** (`terrain/mesh.ts`
   `GroundMaterial`). Its injected GLSL sits after `<color_fragment>` and `<normal_fragment_maps>`.
   `customProgramCacheKey()` is a constant. A wet program therefore needs a constructor option, a define and its
   own cache key, so the shipped program and its cache entry are untouched.
6. **Sway is literal GLSL too.** `SWAY_AMPLITUDE` and `SWAY_GUST_GAIN` are interpolated into `sway.ts`'s chunk.
   `uSwayAmp` is the `wind` debug layer's toggle, with a stash. Writing 1.5 into `uSwayAmp` would fight that stash,
   and the `wind` floor would read the wet gain as its own. So wet sway is a compile-time variant (R-3).
7. **Decal colour is eight palette uniforms** in `DECAL_PALETTE_ORDER` (`decal-maths.ts`): bowl 0, lip 1, scorch 2,
   oil 3, rubble 4/5, tread 6, tyre 7. Tread is `dust.5` on every theme today. Under rain, indices 6 and 7 take
   `mud.3`. These are uniform VALUES, so the decal program stays shipped unless the crater-water define is on.
8. **`vehicle_dust` is picked by NAME** (`ThreeRenderer.updateVehicleAmbientFx`, `emitterLibrary.byName`). No
   trigger-wide dispatch was found for `vehicle_move`. So `vehicle_mud` is a by-name swap, and a second emitter
   with the same trigger fires nothing by itself. Task 5 confirms this against `vfx-triggers.test.ts` first.
9. **Reduced motion is already live.** `RendererOptions.reducedMotion?: () => boolean`. `main.ts` passes
   `prefersReducedMotion` (`ui/motion.ts`: `data-motion="reduce"` or the OS query) at three sites. The renderer asks
   it per frame (the flinch, the wound ring). So reduced motion needs no app edit, and a settings change mid-mission
   takes effect at once.
10. **Debug layers** are a closed list, `DEBUG_LAYERS` (`debug-layers.ts`), with `unknownDebugLayerMessage` naming
    them all. CLAUDE.md's rule applies: a layer whose state a per-frame write re-asserts needs a flag that write
    consults (`unitsDebugHidden`). Rain visibility and the ripple amplitude are both written per frame (reduced
    motion), so both need such a flag.
11. **`render-order.ts` has a table test**, "every band constant has its row in the table". A new
    `RAIN_RENDER_ORDER` needs its row, and the band tests (`FX_RENDER_ORDER_ABOVE` 3 < overlay 4 < smoke 5 <
    silhouette 6) must still hold.
12. **`photographGround`** renders the minimap's ground with its own camera and two targets. The rain batch must be
    hidden there, the way it hides units. The water and the wet tones must draw.
13. **`QUALITY_PRESETS` is pinned with `toEqual`** (`quality.test.ts`). A new optional field is a deliberate pin
    move. `high` stays "today's frame on every shipped map" because no shipped map rains.
14. **`theme-identity.test.ts`'s per-case loop asserts `shadowMapSize` `[4096, 4096]` for EVERY case, the wetland
    fixture included.** Under rain that is `[2048, 2048]` by design, so that assertion is scoped to the shipped
    keys. Its "the wetland fixture draws arid's ground program" assertion flips deliberately (P2 said so).
15. **`perf:fx --breakdown` is the GH-829 instrument** (`tools/src/perf/fx-cost.ts`): one `--map`, readPixels-synced
    `frame(1, 0)`, rounds interleaving configs that each switch ONE thing off (a pass, the shadow pass or a debug
    layer), median, per-round medians and p95, draw calls by hand, the rAF cadence first, refuses SwiftShader
    (exit 2). It lacks: a second map in the same run (the basin reference), the wet layers as configs, a
    shadow-size config, and per-map views. Task 10 adds them.
16. **`ground:capture` freezes the frame loop at boot**, at 1400x900 @1x (`CAPTURE_VIEWPORT`), and does not record the
    tick (P2 finding). The looks need the tick recorded, a puddle A/B within one run, a wading squad, a frame
    sequence and a reduced-motion boot. Tasks 6 and 12 add them.
17. **The rain height texture is 74 KB, not 37 KB.** 4 texels a tile on 48x48 is 192² = 36,864 texels; R16F is
    2 bytes a texel. The spec's "37 KB" is the texel count. Immaterial to the budget; recorded.
18. **The stand-ins draw about 0.533 M decor triangles on `ein_qasab`** (P2 census), not the 0.75 M the real art is
    estimated at. The wet-layer DELTA does not depend on decor, so the budget A/B holds with stand-ins. The
    whole-frame target (decision 13, "a wet map's frame at or below the basin's plus the budget") does, so it is
    reported "with stand-ins" here and re-measured after P3 (Seams).
19. **Control C has no surface to read.** `buildControlMap(input: TerrainInput)` takes no `TerrainSurface`, but the
    open-ground puddle rule needs the drawn normal (`normal.y > 0.985`). The bake takes the surface as a second
    argument (`composeTerrain` has it). `TerrainInput` gains an optional `wet?: true`.
20. **The marsh water already exists as a function.** `marshWaterAt(decor, width, height, x, z)` (`terrain/shared.ts`),
    share 0.3119 on `ein_qasab`; the reed placer rejects a clump centre where it reads `>= 0.5`. Control C's R
    channel samples the same function, so the reeds and the water agree by construction, and a test proves it on the
    texels.
21. **The water proxy, recomputed at planning time** (a hand-written CIEDE2000, D65, the spec 4.2 formula
    `(1-R) x 0.55 x floor + R x overcast.0`, linear). Task 3's test recomputes these with the repo's own helper and
    pins what IT reads; these figures only frame the A/B:

    | Floor | R 0.20 | R 0.25 | R 0.35 | Luminance over the bare floor (0.20 / 0.25 / 0.35) |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | `mud.1` `#725944` (road puddles) | dE 16.6 | 16.8 | 18.5 | 1.47x / 1.70x / 2.16x (the spec's figures) |
    | `mud.2` `#584535` (the marsh floor since S-6) | 16.6 | 16.3 | 17.7 | **2.18x / 2.59x / 3.40x** |

    Every value clears the 15.6 floor against KDF `olive.1`. But **on the lifted marsh floor, water reads 2.2-3.4x the
    floor at every reflectance**, above the spec's 1.5-2x band, which was taken from track puddles (refs 16, 17). So
    puddles and marsh water get one uniform each (R-5), and the A/B shows both (Q3).

## Rulings on ambiguity (made in this plan; the lead may overturn any)

- **R-1. One switch: `opts.weather === 'rain'`.** Control C, `THEME_WET`, `rainLight`, the wet fog, foliage, decal
  and unit values, `vehicle_mud` and the rain batch all key on it. The theme id never reaches the renderer.
- **R-2. `rainLight(lights, preset)`** is pure, in `three/weather.ts`. It takes the `ResolvedLights` the constructor
  built (`DAY_LIGHTS` for day) and the preset's haze fields, and returns new lights plus the haze and shadow
  settings. It keeps the sun DIRECTION, so rain at dawn is still dawn. The numbers (spec 4.5):

  | Quantity | Rule | `day` today | `day` under rain |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | sun intensity | x `RAIN_SUN_SCALE` 0.46, any preset | 2.6 | 1.196 (the spec's table rounds it to 1.2) |
  | sun colour | `overcast.0` | `limestone.0` | `overcast.0` `#C4C8C9` |
  | hemisphere (fill) | x `RAIN_FILL_SCALE` 1.5, any preset (Q2) | 0.9 | 1.35 |
  | sky key | `overcast.1` | `water.0` | `#9FA9B5` |
  | shadow strength | `RAIN_SHADOW` 0.45 (`sun.shadow.intensity`) | 1 | 0.45 |
  | shadow map | one step down, floored at 1024 | 4096 / 2048 / 1024 | 2048 / 1024 / 1024 |
  | `hazeFar` | `RAIN_HAZE_FAR` 0.26 on every preset (spec: max(preset, 0.26), never above 0.26; every preset is <= 0.18) | 0.12 | 0.26 |
  | haze key | `overcast.1`, overriding a preset's own (dusk's `dust.1`) | theme | `#9FA9B5` |
  | `HAZE_LOW` | `RAIN_HAZE_LOW` 0.10 | 0.06 | 0.10 |
  | exposure | unchanged, ACES 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 |

  Open-ground irradiance `(sunI x sun.y + hemiI) / pi`: day 0.964 -> **0.741** under rain (-23%). A mission's
  `lightOverride` is applied AFTER `rainLight` (the mission wins). No mission is wetland, so this is moot today and
  recorded.
- **R-3. Compile-time variants, built under rain only.** Four programs differ on a wet map, and none on a shipped map:
  - the ground program: `THEME_WET` (Task 4);
  - the fog pass: `hazeLow` 0.10 interpolated into its source (Task 2);
  - the foliage material: the wet sway chunk, amplitude x `RAIN_SWAY_AMP` 1.5 and gust `RAIN_SWAY_GUST` 1.8, with
    its own `customProgramCacheKey` (Task 5);
  - the decal program: a `DECAL_WET` define for the crater water (Task 5).

  Plus the rain program (Task 8). The spec counted "+1 (rain), plus one ground permutation". The other three are
  this plan's, chosen so that no shipped program SOURCE changes (Q7). Each costs a compile on a wet map's boot,
  measured in Task 11's load line.
- **R-4. Control C** (RGBA8, `CONTROL_TEXELS_PER_TILE` 8, about 0.75 MiB with mips on 48x48) is built only when
  `TerrainInput.wet` is true:
  - **R:** the marsh water weight, `marshWaterAt` at the texel centre, as is (it already carries its 0.25-tile
    inset and 0.1 ramp);
  - **G:** the puddle mask, the max of three terms, then zeroed by the never-water rules:
    - roads: inside the rut band of control B's road distance, times the rut noise, thresholded to **25-35% of
      road area**;
    - open ground: the macro field thresholded on flat ground (`smoothNormal(...).y > 0.985`), **4-8% of open
      ground**;
    - low ground: the open threshold lowered so the share rises by **+6% at 2 levels below**
      `hazeReferenceLevel`, linearly from 0 at the reference;
    - never water: a terrace top, a `^` top, a building pad, a slope over 10 degrees, a crown;
  - **B, A:** spare, written 0.

  The P2 shoreline's per-vertex mixes STAY (Q4). Control C does not replace them in P4.
- **R-5. Two reflectances, defaulting to 0.25.** `uPuddleReflect` (roads and open ground) and `uMarshReflect`
  (marsh water), both 0.25 until S-L1, when the lead picks each from the A/B at 0.20, 0.25 and 0.35. The water
  surface (spec 4.3) is the wet tone x 0.55, mixed toward `overcast.0` SCENE-REFERRED (its linear value x the same
  `hazeRadiance` factor the haze tint takes under the rain lights) by the reflectance. A 0.06-tile rim of `mud.3`
  edges every water mask.
- **R-6. Ripples.** On water fragments only (`C.R >= 0.5` or `C.G >= 0.5`), on the sim clock, each 0.5-tile cell a
  `tileHash` stream on fresh offsets (grepped unused across `terrain/` and `decal-*.ts`), 2 rings a cell, 0.12-tile
  rings, 0.9 s life, at most an 8-degree normal tilt, and ±15% on the reflection. Off on `low`
  (`RenderQuality.rain.ripples`), off under reduced motion, off with the `ripples` layer hidden.
- **R-7. The rain batch geometry** (`rain-maths.ts`, all pure):
  - **The box is a square of side `P`** centred on the camera focus, where `P` is the smallest of {8, 16, 32, 64}
    tiles that covers the camera's ground footprint padded 10%. Each drop owns a seed `s_i` in `[0,1)^3`. Its world
    x is `lo + mod(s_i.x P - lo, P)` with `lo = focus.x - P/2`, and z likewise: **world-anchored mod P**, so a pan
    moves no drop, and the count on screen is the same at every zoom. Crossing a `P` step reshuffles; that is
    accepted and tested as the only reshuffle.
  - **Fall:** 4.0 wu/s, `y = ground + H - mod(s_y H + 4.0 t, H)` with `H` = 6 wu and `t` = `presentationSimMs / 1000`,
    slanted 12 degrees along the sway wind direction (`SWAY_DIR_X`, `SWAY_DIR_Z`).
  - **Streak:** 0.42 wu long, **1.5 CSS px wide at every zoom** (the constant-pixel rule, DPR included), alpha 0.28.
  - **Splash:** in the same instance and draw, a ring 0.04 -> 0.10 wu over 150 ms after the drop's ground time, alpha
    0.35 -> 0, `overcast.1`.
  - **Ground:** an R16F height texture, 4 texels a tile (74 KB on 48x48, point 17), baked at boot from
    `ground-height.ts`'s `groundWorldY`, under 5 ms.
  - **Colour:** `overcast.0` linear x the rain `hazeRadiance`. Its largest channel must stay under 0.9 on every
    preset under rain, so it never blooms, and `hdr-gains.ts` gets no entry.
  - **Draw:** depth test on, depth write off, no `normal` attribute, `castShadow` false (the decal pool's proof that
    AO and shadow never see it), main pass only, hidden in `photographGround`.
  - **Order:** `RAIN_RENDER_ORDER = FX_RENDER_ORDER_ABOVE`, a named alias (the decal-band pattern): over units, under
    overlays (4), smoke (5) and the silhouette (6).
- **R-8. The preset fields and the ladder.** `RenderQuality.rain?: { drops: number; ripples: boolean; openPuddles:
  boolean }`, absent meaning "no opinion" (a test fake builds no rain fields and gets `high`'s):

  - `medium` `{ drops: 3500, ripples: true, openPuddles: true }`;
  - `low` `{ drops: 1500, ripples: false, openPuddles: true }` (Q8).

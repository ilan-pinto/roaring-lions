# Capture the flag v2: a defended flag with a set-up phase (GH-976). Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task by task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** replace today's flag raid, where each flag stands nearer its raider than its owner, with a version where
your flag stands 6-12 path tiles behind your own seat. Each side gets a 3:00 set-up with a fixed fortification kit,
inside a 30-minute match. The live flag mode stays offered, byte for byte, until the new version replaces it per map.

**Architecture:** the sim enforces the set-up: a clock with an early bell, a region clamp at the seat bisector,
weapons tight, and kit placement through a footprint form of `placementReason`. All of it sits behind a new
`setupLive` hash latch, so no existing pin moves. `@lions/data` computes the regions with exact integers. A new,
room-minted `MatchConfig.flagSetup` switches a room to the new rules. The Worker mints it only for maps in
`@lions/net`'s `FLAG_SETUP_MAPS`, which stays empty until WP9. The new flag data is STAGED in a `flag_setup` block
beside the shipped `flags`, so the live mode never reads it.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript strict, vitest, Ajv (schemas), plain-node `.mjs` validators, the headless versus probe.

**Spec:** `/Users/ilpinto/dev/roaring-lions-ep/_coord/ctf-redesign/design.md` (the design, 6 Nov) and the lead's
rulings, the last comment on GH-976 (6 Nov). Where they disagree the rulings win, and where this plan's §0 rulings
settle a contradiction between the design and the code, §0 wins.

**Base:** `origin/main` `a563ca258` (v0.130.11). Find code by SYMBOL, never by line: `placementReason`,
`footprintSeals`, `addStructure`, `removeStructure`, `recomputeFields`, `applyCommands`, `stepCombat`,
`stepKamikaze`, `stepDemolition`, `stepMovement` (the wall-clip block after `clampX`/`clampY`), `hash()`, `tick()`,

## Global Constraints

- The four invariants (CLAUDE.md): 20 Hz tick; no floating point and no `Math.*`/`Date.*` in `@lions/sim`;
  randomness only from `rng(entityId)`; commands in -> sim -> state + events out. Region masks are computed OUTSIDE
  the sim (`@lions/data`) and handed in as config.
- `pnpm test:determinism` passes before every commit touching `@lions/sim`. A pin moves only deliberately, in the
  same commit, with the reason in the commit message.
- **Set-up:** `setup.seconds` 180 (3:00); early bell `SETUP_BELL_DELAY_TICKS` 100 (5 s) after BOTH sides press
  Ready; flag match cap `FLAG_SETUP_CAP_MINUTES` 30 on every map, set-up included.
- **Band:** `setup.band_tiles` 8 on a 48x48 map, 12 on an 80x80 or 96x96 map. The band is every tile whose centre
  lies less than `band_tiles / 2` from the perpendicular bisector of the two seats.
- **Kit:** `{ hesco: 12, sandbag_post: 4, t_wall: 2 }` per side, placed instantly, set-up only, owned by the placer.
  Slot order `SETUP_KIT_PIECES = ['hesco', 'sandbag_post', 't_wall']`.
- **Field works stay off in versus** (`raiseWorks`/`cancelWorks` keep returning false in `validateCommand`).
- **Rules:** raid and hold, no carry-home. Hold 45 s in one go (`flag_setup.hold_s`; the probe may fit 30-60, one
  value per size class). Only ground units hold or contest (`ground_only`).
- **Flag placement (R1):** 5x5, every tile open (`OPEN_SYMBOLS`); G-ratio own path <= 0.5 x enemy path; G-depth own
  path 6-12, enemy path >= 45 (48x48) or >= 80 (80/96); G-edge zone >= 3 tiles from every map edge. Paths are
  4-neighbour BFS on the foot mask from each seat (`anchorsOf`) to the NEAREST tile of the zone.
- **Set-up rules:** S1 every unit spawns inside its own region; S2 the 11x11 apron round each flag lies inside its
  owner's region; S3 every flag tile is >= 17 tiles (Euclidean, tile centres; 17^2 = 289) from every enemy-region
  tile; S4 band >= 8 (48x48) / >= 12 (80/96).
- **Maps, v1:** wadi_tarfan, milh_flats, rujm_sawda, wadi_tarfan_l, milh_flats_l. tel_marum and
  beit_sahwan_outskirts wait for a separate fit.
- **Probe bars:** G1 leader <= 60% (all games, and decisive games alone); G2 >= 70% decided by the goal (fallback
  60% with the contested-capture share printed); G3 median bell-to-goal 4-10 min (48x48) / 6-15 min (80/96), and
  <= 5% of games at the cap; G4 p10 bell-to-goal >= 2:00 (48) / 3:00 (80/96); G5 RUSH end wins <= 50%; G6 TURTLE end
  wins <= 10%; G7 NOSETUP end loses >= 60%; G8 PASSIVE loses every game by the goal. (The design calls these
  "G1-G7" and lists eight; this plan numbers them G1-G8.)
- **Seeds:** seed n is `1000 + 7919 n`. Fit n = 40000-40191, a 24-seed screen (40000-40023) first; read ONCE on n =
  41000-41287; PR review on n = 42000-42287.
- House rules: never `git add -A` (use `git commit -- <paths>`); never `git checkout <file>`; never `git stash`;
  never pkill/killall; port 5177 is the lead's; no local `ui:routes`/`golden-baseline`/`ui:shots`; at most one
  Metal browser check on your own port with music off (`tools/src/ui-review/music-off.ts`); write issue refs as
  GH-NNN; no deploys.
- Every chrome string through `t()`, keys in `packages/app/src/i18n/en.json`; no colour literals in UI source.

## Review Focus

The five inputs most likely to bite a player that the design does not name. Each one has its test in the task
that owns the code.

1. **A carrier parked on the last tile of its region unloads.** Expected: every passenger lands inside the side's
   region, or the unload is refused. It never puts a man in the band. Test in WP2 (`setup.test.ts`, "unload at the
   line").
2. **A garrison order on a building whose centroid lies in the band or the enemy region**, reachable from your own
   side's door. Expected: refused during set-up. A man inside would stand past the line and fire from there at the
   bell. Test in WP2 ("garrison past the line").
3. **Ready pressed twice, pressed by one side only, or pressed in the last 5 s.** Expected: idempotent per side.
   One side's Ready changes nothing. The bell is never later than `setup.seconds`. Test in WP2 ("ready edge cases").
4. **A snapshot cut on the bell tick itself**, with the bell brought forward by Ready to land on frame 1,199.
   Expected: the restored world rings the bell on the same tick and keeps every hash. Test in WP4 ("rejoin on the
   bell tick").
5. **A room paused during set-up.** Expected: the countdown the banner shows stops, because it is read from the sim
   tick and never from the wall clock. Test in WP7 ("paused countdown").

## §0 Rulings: the design checked against the code

Each item names what the design says, what `origin/main` shows, and what this plan does.

**R0.1 Staging, since WP0 is dropped.** The lead: flag "STAYS offered as it is until the new version replaces it".
So WP1 does NOT touch the shipped `flags` or `modes.flag`. It adds a top-level `flag_setup` block, only on the five
maps: `{ flags, hold_s, setup: { seconds, band_tiles, kit } }`. The switch is per ROOM, not per file: the Worker
mints `MatchConfig.flagSetup: true` for a flag room whose map is in `@lions/net` `FLAG_SETUP_MAPS`. That list stays
`[]` until WP9, and WP9 is the per-map relist. Tools and tests set `flagSetup: true` on a config directly. Every
reader picks its flag block through one helper, `flagBlockOf(file, setup)`. A new pin, `FLAG_V1_GAME_PIN` (WP1),
holds a v1 flag game byte for byte from WP1 to WP9.

**R0.2 `placementReason` needs a `buildable` block: TRUE.** It throws `structure type N is not buildable` without
one, and `hesco`, `sandbag_post` and `t_wall` have none. Do NOT give them one, because the works menu and missions
read `buildable`. Extract a private `footprintRefusal(tx, ty, fw, fh): PlacementRefusal | null`, with the same four
rules in the same order (off_map, blocked, no_footing, not_level, overlap, seals_passage). `placementReason` calls
it with `b.fw, b.fh`. `fortify` calls it with `1, 1`. Field works stay byte-identical: the works and construct tests
and `FORWARD_LINE_PIN` are the witnesses.

**R0.3 "One `recomputeFields` per tick (~9-19 ms a call)": STALE.** GH-913 L2 made every structure op an exact
incremental repair (`beginFlips`/`noteFlip`, `recomputeFields('closed' | 'opened')`). Each accepted `fortify` is
one `addStructure` and each `unfortify` one `removeStructure`, with no batching. WP3 measures and prints, without
gating, the time of 36 placements in one tick on wadi_tarfan_l with the pool warm.

**R0.4 `stepMovement`.** The design says a unit "does not advance". The code has a wall-clip block right after
`clampX`/`clampY` that slides along a blocked axis, and it skips that block for aircraft. Plan: while
`setupActive`, a tile whose `setupRegion` cell is not the unit's side counts as clipped, in the SAME three-branch
slide. It applies to every unit, aircraft INCLUDED, so the region clip sits outside the `!type.isAir` guard. A unit
slides along the line and its order stands (`moving` stays 1). When set-up is not active the existing code runs
untouched: the new branch is a separate `if` on a per-tick local, so non-set-up worlds stay byte-identical.

**R0.5 `stepCombat` is not the only damage path.** Weapons tight needs four guards, each behind a per-tick
`const tight = this.setupActive`:
- `stepCombat`: every unit takes the pinned-branch writes (`engaging = 0`, `curTarget = -1`,
  `curStructure = -1`, `continue`). This also stops the beam and `fireAtStructure`, and an attack-mover does not
  halt to engage.
- `stepKamikaze`: no dive.
- `stepDemolition`: `demoTicks = 0`, `demoTarget = -1`, `continue`. `dozer_d9` has `demolish` and auto-charges
  wherever it halts.
- `applyCommands`: the commands that could damage or leave the board are refused first, before any side effect
  (`SETUP_REFUSED_KINDS`: `smoke`, `demolish`, `chargeTunnel`, `attack`, `enterTunnel`, `vent`, `reveal`,
  `callStrike`, `construct`, `cancelConstruct`).

Detection still runs.

**R0.6 `ground_only`.** `livingIn`, `contestedIn` and `enemyNear` never test the air: confirmed. Plan: an optional
`groundOnly = false` parameter on all three, and a new public `Sim.isAircraft(id): boolean`, the TYPE flag
(`unitTypes[typeIdx].isAir`), landed or not. A landed helicopter holds nothing. `ObjectiveJson.ground_only?:
boolean` is added to the TS type only, NOT to `mission.schema.json`, whose objective items are
`additionalProperties: false`, so no authored mission can set it. `versusModeMissionOf` sets it only with
`{ setup: true }`: a v1 room keeps aircraft holding, as it does today.

**R0.7 "Flag objectives wait for the bell"** moves from the design's WP4 to WP3. The code is `stepGoals` in
`packages/sim/src/mission.ts`, sim-package code. While `sim.setupActive`, `stepGoals` skips `stepObjectives`. That
leaves WP4 app and net only.

**R0.8 The cap is not file data.** The Worker imports `@lions/net` and not `@lions/data`. So the design's
`modes.flag.cap_minutes` becomes two constants, `FLAG_SETUP_CAP_MINUTES = 30` in `@lions/data` and in `@lions/net`,
pinned equal by a test. A new `matchCapTicksOf(config)` replaces `matchCapTicks(config.map)` wherever a config is in
hand (`outcome.ts` `hashTickOutcome`, `arena-setup.ts`, the probes, `room-core.ts` via a config-aware
`maxLogCmdChars`). The large maps' flag cap goes from 35 to 30 minutes in a set-up room only.

**R0.9 Command naming.** The sim command is `setupReady`, not `ready`, because the wire already has
`{ t: 'ready' }` (lobby) and `SeatState.ready`. The kit commands are `fortify` and `unfortify`. Adding a kind to
`Command` breaks `tsc` in `COMMAND_KINDS`, `cleanCommand` and `validateCommand` (none has a default branch). So
the sim PR that adds a kind also adds:
- the kind to `COMMAND_KINDS`;
- a `cleanCommand` case returning `null`;
- a `validateCommand` case returning `false`;
- an `ORDER_WITHHELD` entry (`legal: false`, why: `set-up commands open in WP4`).

WP4 opens the wire, and WP8 moves the three into `ORDER_VERBS`.

**R0.10 Structure ids are never reused.** `MAX_STRUCTURES` is 256, `addStructure` only increments, and
`removeStructure` frees no id. Unlimited fortify/unfortify churn would exhaust the table. Plan:
`SETUP_PLACEMENTS_PER_SIDE = 36` (twice the kit) lifetime accepted `fortify` per side, refused after with
`placements_spent`. WP4's test asserts map structures + 2 x 36 <= 256 on every `flag_setup` map. rujm_sawda stands
about 156 map structures (pre-flight count of symbol runs), so it fits with room to spare.

**R0.11 `removeStructure` throws on occupants.** `unfortify` of a garrisoned `sandbag_post` is refused with
`occupied`.

**R0.12 An owned `addStructure` sets `fwLive`.** That folds the FW block and turns on `sensorObservations` and
`latchOwnedStructures` in a set-up world. This is wanted: owned kit pieces become targets once seen, as the design
says. It moves no pin, because no pinned world fortifies.

**R0.13 Sight crosses the band.** Ground sight reaches 12 and the band is 8 on 48x48, so each side can see the
other's front during set-up. The design accepts this ("fog is drawn by the renderer and stops nothing"). S3 keeps
every flag beyond air sight (16) from the enemy region.

**R0.14 HUD keys.** The catalogue's namespace is `versus.flag.*`, not the design's `hud.flag.*`. New keys go under
`versus.flag.defend`, `versus.flag.raid` and `versus.setup.*`.

**R0.15 The dashed line reuses `supplyRoutes`.** `RendererState.supplyRoutes` draws a ground-draped ribbon with a
dashed `cutSpan`. WP7 writes the band's centre line as one route whose whole span is cut (dashed), during set-up
only. That means no `packages/render` edit and no visual-gate movement. The design's own-region TINT is dropped: the
dashed line, the banner and the "Holding at the line" chip carry it. If the lead's look at the WP7 still says
otherwise, a render task is opened then (§ Lead review).

**R0.16 Path figures.** The design's "51 vs 27" for wadi_tarfan_l is a flag-CENTRE distance. The validator's BFS to
the nearest zone tile reads 68 / 34 on the shipped wadi_tarfan_l friendly flag, ratio 2.0. G-ratio is red on it
either way, and that is WP1's falsification. Pre-flight also confirms the rules can be met on all five maps (see
WP1, Step 5).

**R0.17 After WP9.** A flag room on a listed map always plays the set-up rules. tel_marum, beit_sahwan_outskirts and
any of the five maps that fails the bars leave `WIN_MODE_FAIR_MAPS.flag` at WP9, because their v1 layout is the
flag-behind-the-enemy-line fault the lead raised. This is the default unless the lead rules otherwise at the review
point. Folding `flag_setup` into `flags`/`modes.flag` and deleting the v1 branch is a follow-up issue filed at WP9,
not part of this plan.

The region rule, exact (`setupRegionOf` and its `.mjs` twin `setupRegion(map, anchors, band)`):
- Doubled coordinates: tile centre C = (2x+1, 2y+1), A' = (2ax+1, 2ay+1), B' = (2bx+1, 2by+1).
- D = |C-B'|^2 - |C-A'|^2 and L = |A'-B'|^2.
- The cell is 0 when D > 0 and D^2 >= 4·band^2·L, 1 when D < 0 and D^2 >= 4·band^2·L, and -1 otherwise.

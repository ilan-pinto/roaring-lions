# Net N5, co-op against a mission: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Two friends join one room. Both are KDF on side 0, with matched base kit. They pick any shipped
mission except the tutorial and play it against its own scripted enemy, waves and triggers. Each commands
their own half of the force. The room ends on the mission's own result, and the debrief shows the
objectives and the grade. **Nothing is written to the campaign ledger, the brigade account, the saves,
the tutorial flag or the credits.** A partner who drops for 60 s hands their units to the one who stayed,
and gets them back on rejoin. A reload mid-mission rejoins through the snapshot path.

**Architecture:**
- **`packages/net`** gains:
  - `mode: 'coop'` and `MatchConfig.mission`;
  - `coop.ts`, holding the mission list (`COOP_MISSIONS`, id to map), the seat table (`CoopSeats`), the
    co-op digest fold and the snapshot's seat block;
  - an optional `sys` list on a `Frame`: the room's handover and handback events, which both clients apply
    on the same tick;
  - a seat-aware `own` in `validateCommand`.
- **`packages/worker/src/match`**:
  - the route and the room accept a co-op create;
  - `mintConfig` mints two side-0 KDF seats on the single-player seed;
  - `room-core` replaces the versus forfeit with the 60 s handover in a co-op room;
  - the room relays a co-op `ping`.
- **`packages/app/src/net`**:
  - `coop-world.ts` holds `buildCoopWorld(config)`, the ONE function that boots a mission for a room (the
    browser and the AI harness both call it);
  - a `worldFor(config)` dispatcher, used by the snapshot producer and the rejoin;
  - `coop-end.ts` is the pure "what a mission end writes" plan, which writes nothing in a co-op room.
- **`packages/app`**:
  - the battlefield boots `/mission/<id>?net=<code>`;
  - selection takes the units this seat controls;
  - the partner's units draw in a new team colour;
  - the lobby gains "Play a mission together";
  - the debrief writes nothing.
- **`packages/render`** gains one optional `Renderer` method, `setControlledBy()`, which is presentation only.
- **`data/palette.json`** gains `team.partner` in all four colour-vision sets.
- **`tools`** gains:
  - co-op streams in `two-client.test.ts`;
  - a co-op arm in the snapshot witness;
  - co-op support in `AgentClient`;
  - a relay end-to-end handover test;
  - a report-only two-plan playtest arm;
  - the one Metal instrument.

**Expected to change no file under `packages/sim/` and to move no sim pin.** A task that finds it must
touch the sim STOPS and raises it (G-1).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict, vitest, `@lions/net`, `@lions/sim` (read only: `MissionRuntime`,
`encodeSim`, `restoreSim`, `SnapshotRefused`), `@lions/data`, the Worker's `fake-do.ts` and
`tools/src/net/local-relay.ts`. For the one browser run, Playwright, which is already a tools dependency.
No new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-network-next.md` §4 (N5) and §5 decisions 1–6. The lead
approved N5 on 28 Oct, and decisions 2–6 stand as recommended:
- **2:** the force is split by placement, alternating; each reinforcement goes to the seat with fewer
  living units, ties to seat 0;
- **3:** matched base kit, every shipped mission except the tutorial, an empty ledger, no rebalance;
- **4:** after 60 s a dropped partner's units pass to the remaining seat, the mission goes on, and a rejoin
  hands them back. There is no forfeit;
- **5:** fire support, field works and the Conduct score are shared, and either seat may call support;
- **6:** no payout.

These bind too:

## What the code says, and what it changes about the spec's picture

Each point was checked against `c5f34ecd`. Read them before any task.

1. **A mission is refused in a room today.** `bootBattlefield` throws `'a room runs Free Play only'` when
   `?net=` meets a `missionId`. The single-player mission boot is inline in `main.ts`:
   - the sim is built ~line 1890;
   - `startMission(sim, resolvedMission, ctx, renderer)` runs after the deploy screen, ~line 2719;
   - its `ctx` has eight keys: `typeIdOf`, `markers`, `zones`, `roadMask`, `tunnels`, `ledger`, `loaned`,
     `loanedReinforce` and `unitInfo`.

   `tools/src/mission-harness.ts`' `missionWorld(id, ledger, seed)` says it builds the world "the way the app
   does". It is the oracle for Task 3.
2. **The seed.** `match-room.ts` mints `SIM_SEED_N0 = 20260727` for every room that is not versus. That is
   `main.ts`'s single-player `SIM_SEED`. So a co-op room on that seed boots the single-player world byte for
   byte: ledger `{}`, base kit, no deploy picks, `humanHeld [true, false]` (the default branch of
   `seatRules`).
3. **The snapshot is sim-only, and the seat table is not in it.**
   - `encodeSim(sim, meta, runtime)` writes the sim and the runtime, and `restoreSim` refuses trailing bytes
     (`snapshot.ts`: `'trailing bytes'`).
   - The split of the starting force is a function of the mission JSON, so it is recomputable.
   - Decision 2's reinforcement rule ("fewer living units" at the spawn tick) and the handover state are
     **history**. No sim column records a spawn or death tick, so neither can be rebuilt from the state at T.
   - So the table must travel with the snapshot. Ruling 9: a seat block after the sim bytes, covered by the
     room's existing SHA pair, and stripped by the client before `restoreSim`.
4. **The room only reads the header.** `snapshot-intake.ts` (`admitTick`, `PendingBook`, `decide`) and
   `readSnapshotHeader` read `format`, `matchDigest` and `frameTick` and nothing past them. The room
   compares the two seats' uploads by the SHA-256 of the whole inflated body. **So the seat block needs no
   Worker change to be stored and verified.** Task 0 confirms it by reading `match-room.ts`'s snapshot POST
   path. If the room decodes past the header, STOP (S-0).
5. **A dropped seat freezes the room.** `stallFor` stalls on a `gone` or `behind` seat, and `forfeitApplies`
   is versus-only. So a co-op room today would freeze until `expire`. The handover (Task 7) must also stop
   a handed-over seat from stalling the room.
6. **`validateCommand` knows sides, not seats.** `own(id)` is `sideOfEntity(id) === sideOfSeat(seat)`. The
   kinds priced only by the mission runtime are gated on `!world.versus`:
   - `support`, `callStrike`, `reveal`, `raiseWorks` and `cancelWorks`;
   - `build`, `cancelBuild`, `setRally` and `clearRally`, which also pass on `world.economy`.

   A co-op room is not versus, so those pass as decision 5 wants. Only `own` needs the seat.
7. **The content digest does not cover missions.** `content-digest.ts` folds units, structures, maps,
   doctrines and versusRules. Adding missions there would move every versus room's reported digest. Ruling
   8 folds the room's own mission into the co-op digest instead, so no versus number moves.
8. **The snapshot witness has never seen a campaign mission.** `SNAPSHOT_COVERAGE_EXEMPT` lists every runtime
   row only a mission moves, with the reason "no witness world has …":
   - triggers, waves, the end, civilians, ROE, field works, groups, tags and the roster pool.
   - The co-op arm (Task 10) is the first witness that moves them.
   - The census fails on an exempt row that moves, so those entries are **deleted** in that commit. This is
     expected.
   - It is also the first real test of S2's runtime restore on mission state, hence STOP S-3.
9. **Thirty-one `mode` reads.** `mode === 'versus'` and `mode !== 'versus'` (and the sandbox forms) appear at:
   - 11 sites in `match-room.ts`, 4 in `routes.ts` and 1 in `room-core.ts`;
   - 2 in `config.ts`, 1 in `ownership.ts` and 1 in `seat-rules.ts`;
   - in the app: 2 in `main.ts`, and 1 each in `connect.ts`, `frame-step.ts`, `match-world.ts`,
     `scoreboard.ts`, `snapshot-gate.ts` and `snapshot-producer.ts`, with 2 in `snapshot-rejoin.ts`.

   **Several read "not versus" as "sandbox"**: `match-room.ts`'s meta reader writes
   `m.mode === 'versus' ? 'versus' : 'sandbox'`, and so does `routes.ts`' create. Each site is classified in
   Task 0, and a co-op config must never fall into a sandbox branch.
10. **Selection is the app's.** `renderer.selection` is written only by `main.ts`, through about 20
    `isOwnSide(sim.state.side[i], me)` sites: click, box, select-all, idle, the construct builders, the
    HUD cards and the order sinks. The renderer tints by `colorSlot(side, viewer)` into a three-colour
    `teamColors`.
11. **A mission's text is data with a locale overlay** (`applyMissionLocale`). The runtime must be built
    from the raw `@lions/data` mission, never the overlay. Two seats on two languages must agree.
12. **Missions with production.** 19 missions carry `resources` (a logistics purse), and 7 stand a side-0
    camp. Production spawns land through `runtime.step()` after `start()`, like reinforcements. A `built`
    event carries no entity id.

    accessor, STOP and report (S-2, S-3).

    - the R-N12 row bounds and `bot-row-budget.test.ts`;
    - the visual baselines.

    If one moves, STOP and report.
  - `pnpm test:determinism` runs before every commit, because `net` and `app` import `sim`.
- **G-2. Invariant 4.** The seat table and the handover live outside the sim. They only decide which
  commands reach `queueCommand`, so they are input, never state writes.

  - `restoreSim` is the only way snapshot bytes reach the sim.

  `packages/app/src/net/`, never a copy in `tools/`. This covers `buildCoopWorld`, `coopTick`, `worldFor`,

- **G-4. Determinism of everything that gates a command.** The seat table is derived from these and nothing

  - the raw mission JSON;
  - the sim's hashed columns (`side`, `alive`, `entityCount`);

## Rulings on ambiguity (made in this plan; the lead may overturn any)

1. **The config.**
   - `mode: 'coop'`, plus `mission: string`, present if and only if the mode is coop.
   - `map` must equal `COOP_MISSIONS[mission]`.
   - Both seats are `side: 0`, `faction: 'kdf'`, `kit: 'matched'`, `tiers: {}` and `unlocks: []`.
   - `validateConfig` refuses any other shape, so a stored or relayed config cannot field a brigade kit or a
     Sarim seat.
   - The snapshot cadence is on (`SNAPSHOT_EVERY_TICKS`), as in versus.
2. **The mission list lives in `@lions/net`**, as `COOP_MISSIONS: Readonly<Record<string, string>>` (mission
   id to `map.file`).
   - The reason: `net` and the Worker cannot import `@lions/data`, the same reason `VERSUS_FAIR_MAPS` and
     `SHIPPED_MAP_IDS` are plain lists.
   - It lists every shipped mission except `beit_sahwan_0_tutorial`: 26 today.
   - `tools/src/net/coop-missions.test.ts` pins it both ways against `@lions/data`'s `missions`, and pins the
     tutorial's absence by name. `coopMissionAllowed(id)` reads own keys only.
   - The route and the room refuse anything else with a 400, before a room exists.
3. **The seed is the single-player seed** (`SIM_SEED_N0`, which already equals `main.ts`'s `SIM_SEED`). So a
   co-op boot is the measured single-player boot, which is what "no rebalance" means in practice.
   - Lead Q5 offers a random seed instead.
4. **The world is built from the raw mission.** `buildCoopWorld(config)`:
   - reads `missions[config.mission]` from `@lions/data`, never the locale overlay;
   - builds the sim on `config.seed`, `capacity: 256` and `humanHeld: seatRules(config).humanHeld`;
   - applies `applyTerrain` and `standMapStructures`, and registers the map's tunnels positionally;
   - registers the base-ladder prepass (`upgradePrepass(roster, {})`);
   - resolves the mission with `resolveUpgrades(mission, {}, unlockOf)` and the `bought: false` gates;
   - computes `loanedPlacements` and `loanedReinforcements` from `{}`;
   - builds the eight-key `ctx` exactly as `main.ts` does;
   - calls `new MissionRuntime(...)` and `start()`.

   It **skips the deploy screen's picks**: no `deployedLedger` permutation, because the ledger is empty. It
   returns a `MatchWorld` with `coop: { mission, resolvedMission, seats }`. `main.ts` displays the overlay's
   text for that mission id.
5. **The split** (decision 2).
   - **Start.** `starting_force` entry k goes to seat `k % 2`, whole: the carrier and its passengers together.
     - From an empty ledger no roster is drawn (`spawnPlacement`: `hasRoster` is false), so entry k spawns
       exactly `count × (1 + Σ passengers[j].count)` entities, contiguously and in order.
     - `coopStartSplit(resolvedMission, sim, startingCount)` derives the id ranges and self-checks them:
       the sum equals `runtime.startingCount`, every id in range is side 0, and each range's type sequence
       is the placement's (`unit` × count, each followed by its passengers).
     - A mismatch throws `CoopSplitError(missionId, k)`. It never splits silently.
     - Task 4's test runs the check on all 26 missions. A mission it cannot split exactly is **STOP S-2**.
   - **After start.** Each tick, after the tick body, the new side-0 ids of that tick form one batch: ids in
     `[scanned, entityCount)` with `side === 0`.
     - The batch goes whole to the seat that **owns** fewer living side-0 units, counting `alive === 1`.
       Ties go to seat 0.
     - The count is by owner, never by the handover's controller.
     - This covers `reinforce` triggers, production and anything else the runtime fields. A built unit is a
       reinforcement (lead Q1).
   - Ids on other sides are never owned (`0xff`).
6. **Handover and handback** (decision 4). These are room-decided and stamped into the frame log as `sys`
   events, so both clients flip on the same tick and a full replay reproduces them.
   - `HANDOVER_MS = 60_000` is named apart from `FORFEIT_MS`, though it has the same value.
   - **Handover:** a co-op seat disconnected for `HANDOVER_MS` of wall time. A pause does not stop the clock,
     as with the forfeit. A `bye` hands over at the next frame, with no wait.
   - **Handback:** the seat is connected again, and its `lastAck >= st.tick - 1 - bufferTicks`, so it is
     caught up. The handback is stamped in the next frame.
   - **While handed over**, the seat stalls nothing: neither `gone` nor `behind`. The partner plays on while
     the rejoiner catches up.
   - **Both seats gone:** there is nobody to receive, so the room stays stalled until it expires as it does
     today, and no handover is minted.
   - **Cap:** at most `MAX_SYS_EVENTS` (64) per room. Past it the room mints none, and units stay with
     whoever controls them. A test holds the cap.
   - `controllerOf(id)` is the owner, unless the owner is handed over; then it is the other seat.
7. **What is shared** (decision 5).
   - **Shared:** a side-scoped command passes from either seat. These are `build`, `cancelBuild`,
     `setRally`, `clearRally`, `support`, `reveal` and `cancelWorks`, all with `side === 0`.
   - **Controlled by the sender:** a unit-naming command requires every id to be controlled by the sender.
     So do `callStrike`'s `caller`, `raiseWorks`' builders and `load`'s `carrier` (lead Q2).
   - **Any target:** `attack` takes any other-side target, and `rescue` any existing target.
   - **Structures:** `garrison` and `demolish` name structures, which belong to no seat.
   - The Conduct score, the purse, intel and the fire-support cooldowns are the runtime's side-0 state, so
     they are shared by construction.
8. **The co-op digest.**
   - A co-op client reports
     `matchDigest(sim.hash(), coopDigest(runtime.digest(), seats.digest(), missionDigest), contentDigest)`.
   - `missionDigest` is `fnv1a32(canonicalJson(rawMission))`, computed once at boot.
   - `coopDigest` is an FNV-style mix of three u32s, in `@lions/net`.
   - Versus and sandbox call the existing composition untouched, so no versus number moves.
   - The snapshot header's `matchDigest` carries the same co-op number. The post-restore check computes it
     the same way.
9. **The co-op snapshot**, as a byte layout: `encodeSim(sim, meta, runtime)` `‖` seat block `‖` footer.
   - The **footer** is `u32 blockBytes` then the 4-byte magic `'RLCO'`.
   - The **seat block** is `u16 version (1)`, `u32 n`, `n × u8 owner` (`0`, `1` or `0xff`),
     `u8 handedOver[2]` and `u32 scanned`.
   - **The producer** appends it in a co-op room only.
   - **On rejoin, the client:**
     - verifies the SHA over the whole body, as today;
     - finds the footer at the end and strips the block;
     - calls `restoreSim` on the sim part;
     - decodes the block, checking that `n` equals the restored `entityCount` and that every owner is legal;
     - checks the co-op digest.
   - **Refusals:** a co-op config whose snapshot has no footer, a non-co-op config whose snapshot has one, a
     bad version and a bad length are each refused (`coop_block`). They fall back to the full replay
     (S4's G-2), and a refusal is never an error a player sees.
   - The Worker is unchanged (point 4).
10. **The end.**
    - The runtime's result ends the match. `stopAfter` returns true on the frame whose runtime step emitted
      `missionEnd`, so both seats stop on the same tick with the same `result` and `defeatCause`.
    - The seat reports `over(tick)` (L-1), and the room closes `OVER_CLOSE_MS` after both report.
    - **No surrender in co-op.** The room ignores a `surrender` from a co-op seat, and the pause menu offers
      Leave, which is a `bye` and therefore an immediate handover (lead Q3).
    - When both seats have left, the room ends `left`. A `left` names no loser in co-op.
11. **Write nothing.**
    - `coop-end.ts`' pure `missionEndWrites(roomMode)` returns a plan:
      `{ ledger, account, payout, tutorialFlag, saves, nextMission, promotion, campaignTelemetry }`. It is all
      false for co-op, and today's values for single-player.
    - `main.ts`'s victory block reads the plan at each write site and gates nothing else, so single-player is
      byte-for-byte today's path.
    - The debrief takes `writeNothing: true`. That hides "next", the credits line and the promotion, and its
      buttons are "Play again" (`routes.versus()`) and "Main menu".
12. **The lobby.**
    - `/versus` gains a choice between "Against a friend" (today) and "Play a mission together".
    - The co-op choice shows a mission picker over `COOP_MISSIONS ∩ missions`, in campaign order
      (`world.json`). Each card shows the name, the town and `target_minutes`.
    - The room's lobby shows the mission card and its briefing to both seats. The `lobby` message gains
      `mission?: string`, and the client renders its own data for that id.
    - Start navigates to `routes.missionRoom(id, code)`, which is `/mission/<id>?net=<code>`.
13. **The battlefield.**
    - `/mission/:id?net=<code>` boots only when the room's config is co-op and `config.mission === id`.
      Otherwise it shows the existing mismatch error, naming the right link through `routes`.
    - There is no deploy screen, no briefing hold (the frames are the clock, R33), no tutorial and no
      `?fresh`. Every sandbox flag is ignored with today's warning.
14. **Selection takes what this seat controls.**
    - Click, box, double-click-type, select-all, the idle button, control groups, construct builders and
      every order sink filter by `controlledByMe(i)`. That is `isOwnSide && seats.controllerOf(i) === mySeat`,
      and simply `isOwnSide` outside co-op.
    - Information sites keep the side: fog (shared side 0), the minimap's presence, alerts, the feed and
      voice.
    - Task 13 classifies every site in a table in the ledger. An Opus reviewer checks it.
15. **The partner colour follows the controller** (lead Q7). Your own colour marks what you can order, and
    the partner colour marks the rest of side 0.
    - It is a new key `team.partner` in `reserved.team.colors` and in each of the three variants.
    - The CVD gate (`tools/src/cvd.test.ts`) is extended to the fourth colour: ΔE ≥ 25 from `kedem`, `hostile`
      and `neutral` under each variant's own deficiency.
    - `validate_data`'s colour count walks it, and `theme.css` maps `--partner` for the HUD.
    - The renderer gains `setControlledBy?(controlled: (id: number) => boolean)`. It is optional and only
      called in co-op, so no baseline moves. The palette's hex picks are art numbers (lead Q4).
16. **Known gaps, reported and not fixed.** After a snapshot rejoin, the rejoiner's debrief has no mission
    log from before T: the fallen named list and the feed. Its explored-ground memory is lost too (S4
    Ruling 11). Objectives, stars and the Conduct score are runtime state, so they are identical (lead Q6).

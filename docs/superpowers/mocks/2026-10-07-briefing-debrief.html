Briefing, deploy and debrief: three directions

GH-417, polish lane pass H (H1 briefing, H2 deployment, H4 mission end, H5 debrief). These are mock-ups for the lead to choose from. Nothing is built yet. Every frame is drawn at 1400 × 900, the smallest audited size, and every frame fits it with no page scroll and no nested scroll box. The mission is the one in the lead's screenshot, Wadi Halam V: Break the Depot, with the same cumulative roster ("119 in reserve").

The ground in every frame is a real photograph of the 3D map. It comes from the shipped renderer's captureGroundAlbedo (the photographGround path the minimap already uses). It was taken from the running game at commit 62a7f3d3, rows flipped as minimap.ts's flipRows does. The markings on top are drawn from the mission's own markers and zones (data/maps/wadi_halam_5.json, data/missions/wadi_halam_5_depot.json). The audit and the decisions are in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-briefing-debrief-directions.md.

Recommendation: Direction A, "Field order", with B's map pins added to its ground view. A is the only direction that answers all five H1 questions and the H2 force decision on one screen at 1400 × 900, with no scroll anywhere and one primary action. It reuses shipped parts: the objectives panel, the HUD card portraits, the deploy selection rules and the minimap's photograph. B looks best but needs a camera and layout per map. C reads best but adds a step before every mission.

Direction A: Field order

An operation order on one sheet. The left column is the order and the right is the ground. The force runs along the bottom as a row of slots, with the one primary action at the end of that row, where the eye finishes. The five H1 questions get a fixed five-row card. Every row is derived from data the mission already declares, so nothing needs authoring. Where: map name, time of day, target minutes. What happened: the briefing's first beat. Objective: the primaries. What matters: the carrying secondaries and the Conduct floor. Avoid: flagged ROE zones and objective deadlines. The full briefing is still one click away ("Full orders"), as a panel that covers the map rather than a scroll box inside a scroll box.

Direction B: Map table

The lit ground is the screen. The orders and the force float over it as commander-HUD panels in the same band, frame and tile vocabulary as the in-mission HUD (#364), so the briefing reads as the first minute of the mission rather than a document before it. Objectives, threats and the no-fire zone are pinned where they are on the ground. The debrief returns to the same table and pins where things happened. Cost: each map needs a framed crop (the ground is larger than the screen), pin placement that avoids collisions, and recorded positions for the debrief pins. Those positions are app-side, but they are new.

Direction C: Dossier, two steps

The commander delivers the orders, and the force is chosen on a page of its own. Step 1 is the briefing: a full-height portrait and the beats as the commander's speech, one highlighted at a time, already the beat model in loading.ts. Step 2 is the force: one column per demanded type, candidates ranked by record, and a consequences panel. The debrief is a roll call. Cost: one extra click before every mission, and the map is smaller. It is the best fit if the voice-over of the beats (pass F) lands.

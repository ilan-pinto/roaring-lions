Difficulty settings: mock

Three places the player meets it: the new-campaign choice, the campaign board (changeable between missions), and the after-action line that says what the setting did to the grade and the pay. Illustrative names and numbers.

1 · New campaign, after the name
Posture
How hard the campaign pushes back. Change it between missions, never during one.
Easier
Mission: a fifth more time before the clock ends it; enemy waves a quarter thinner and half a minute later; a rifle squad and a medic squad arrive at 1:30.
Grade and pay: no third star; no pay for carried secondaries; no challenge diamond. Conduct and the second star are unchanged.
Standard
Mission: as written.
Grade and pay: every mark and every credit as written.
Harder
Mission: enemy waves a quarter heavier and a quarter-minute earlier. Clock, force and Conduct unchanged.
Grade and pay: the same as Standard. It pays nothing extra.
Credits are never sold, and no posture pays more than Standard.

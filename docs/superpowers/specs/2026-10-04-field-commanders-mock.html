Every other default is accepted (see "Decisions for the lead"), and Buy is refused while an officer is already earned. §3 keeps A, C and D as the record of what was not chosen.
Status lives on the HUD and the icons. No badge, star, name plate or tint is drawn over an officer in the world. He is a unit with a team ring like every other.
The world vocabulary is the one that shipped: the faint team ring, the selected ring (#354), contact marks (#354), the #342 pulse, and FW's dashed aura ring (treatment B).
A world effect is mocked before it is built. §3 offers four treatments of an order's radius, including none at all.

1. The HUD commander slot

This is a fixed card beside the order row in the bottom-centre cluster (spec §5.1). It appears only when the mission has a slot and an officer is fielded, so no gated golden scenario moves. Q selects him, and a second Q centres the camera. Z and X fire his orders. Each order button carries charge pips, a cooldown sweep with the seconds left, and its hotkey. The glyph takes its order family's colour, as the order row's glyphs already do.

2. The selected-unit card with a commander attached

An officer is his own small team (a command tank for Ronen), not a hero welded onto a squad. "Attached" therefore means two things, and both are shown on icons and cards only. Recipients inside his radius carry a chip naming him, and a dug-in squad carries a "Dug in" chip. The officer himself carries the rank tab on his selection chip and on his card's frame. The tab is a chevron in --commend, chosen so it cannot be confused with kit's Stars of David.

3. An order's radius in the world: B chosen (4 Oct)

Each option is drawn at the same moment: Maya is selected among four friendly squads, three of them within her 5-tile Presence and one outside it, with two observed hostiles. The scenes are dimetric (a ground circle draws at 2:1) at about zoom 1, with one tile at 16 px. Every option keeps the shipped vocabulary as it is. Team rings stay at 0.55 alpha and 1.5 px. The selected ring stays team colour, at 0.95 alpha on a shadow.1 halo. Contact marks stay hostile chevrons. None of them puts anything on the officer except the selected ring he already gets. The lead chose B on 4 Oct. A, C and D stay below, marked not chosen.

Option B for the other three officers, and the armed states

Option A's dashed ring is gone, so every case below is said in B's terms. A ring lifts (0.95 alpha, 2.5 px) when its unit is affected. A ring that would be affected but cannot be keeps its faint weight, or takes the no-sight tint where the lead asked for one. No radius is ever drawn. An armed order still owns the next map click (FW §6), and Escape or a right-click disarms it (#268). Where B cannot carry a case, the caption says so and names the smallest addition.

4. Garage and deploy: earning, buying, sitting out

The garage gets an "Officers" section beside the unit tracks (spec §5.3). The sentence on each card is unlockReason's own, and the buy button is buyUnlock's. Earning opens an officer for this campaign; buying opens him for every campaign, and the card says so, because permanence is what the price buys (G-NUM §8.2). Credits only, never Roar coins.

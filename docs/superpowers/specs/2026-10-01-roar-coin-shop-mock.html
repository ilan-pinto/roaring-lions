Roar coin shop: mock-ups (GH-317)

Doc mock for 2026-10-01-roar-coin-shop-design.md, revised to the lead's 1 Oct rulings: coins buy anything credits buy and replace the credit cost, and network play uses only what was earned. It shows the Stores tab in every item state with both prices, the confirm, receipts, the coin at 16/24/48 px, and the three emblem directions. The rate is 1 Roar coin = 10 credits (recommended). Figures come from the 2026-10-01 pnpm playtest ladder: 5,849 credits over 26 missions, a mean of 225 a mission.

2 · The coin at 16 / 24 / 48 px (Direction B, recommended)

Each size is its own drawing rather than a scaled copy. At 16 px it is the hex, one silhouette and the mouth cut. 24 px adds the rim, mane and eye. 48 px adds the relief, the teeth and the glint on the sun's side.

300 Roar coins 300 Roar coins
Beside credits, with a different shape and colour: 3,200 credits  ·  300 Roar coins

Purchase animation (≤ 700 ms)

The coin drops (120 ms), turns one hex face of 60° (250 ms) and is swept by a glint (160 ms). The card then flips to Bought with coins (170 ms), and its credit figure does not move. Reduced motion: an instant state change. Sound: a struck copper coin with a low growl tail, ≤ 600 ms.

3 · Three emblem directions

A · Roaring Roundel

A front three-quarter face with the mane as a notched rim. Rich at 48 px. At 16 px it becomes a brown disc with a hole, and the roundel is the Ari'im patch's own frame.

B · Hex Seal (recommended)

A profile lion roaring, on the campaign board's hexagon. The profile survives 16 px. It is none of the patch's frames (roundel, shield, tab) and cannot be read as a star.

C · Mane Burst

An abstract roar: rays around an open jaw. The most legible at 16 px, but rays around a centre read as a star or a medal, which is the earned register (ST8 T6).

Palette keys used: terracotta.0–2 (coin), limestone.1 (glint and face), shadow.1 (mouth), and the theme.css panel tokens. The only hex values are in the --rl-* block, which mirrors data/palette.json.
